📌 Timesheet Tips That Actually Make a Difference

Log hours daily, not at the end of the week

People who fill in timesheets on Friday afternoon routinely undercount by 10–15%. Logging each day takes 30 seconds and makes your records defensible.

Break work into projects, not just "hours"

Clients and managers value visibility. Separating your time by project or task shows exactly where effort went — and makes billing disputes disappear.

Include the hourly rate when billing

If you're billing by the hour, add the rate so the total pay calculates automatically. The client sees the math. No surprises, no back-and-forth.

Get it signed before you submit

Both employee and supervisor signatures make the timesheet a binding record. This matters when disputes arise months later — and they do, especially with contractors.
