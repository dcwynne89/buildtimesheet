Privacy Policy

Effective: June 7, 2026

1. What We Collect

When you use the free timesheet generator without signing in, we collect nothing. Your timesheet data (names, hours, projects) stays entirely in your browser. No data is sent to our servers.

2. Signed-In Users

If you choose to sign in (via Google or email), we store:

  • Your email address and display name (from your authentication provider)
  • Timesheet data you explicitly save (project names, hours, pay period dates)
  • Timestamps of when documents were created and last modified

This data is stored in Firebase/Firestore and is accessible only to your authenticated account.

3. Analytics & Ads

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ad Settings.

4. Cookies

BuildTimesheet itself does not set cookies. Third-party services (Google AdSense, Firebase Auth) may set cookies for authentication and ad personalization.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Your timesheet data is yours.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete any saved timesheet at any time from the saved timesheets panel. To delete your entire account, email buildpdf.help@gmail.com and we'll remove all associated data within 72 hours.

7. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Authentication is handled by Firebase Auth. We follow industry-standard security practices.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as needed. Continued use of BuildTimesheet after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Questions? buildpdf.help@gmail.com
